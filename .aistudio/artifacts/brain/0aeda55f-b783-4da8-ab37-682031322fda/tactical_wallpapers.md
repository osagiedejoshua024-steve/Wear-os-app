## How to Select Your Wallpaper in the App

1. **On the Smartwatch Simulator**:
   - Locate the top hardware controls above the smartwatch dial.
   - Tap the **`Palette / Wallpaper`** button (highlighted with an emerald brush icon).
2. **Inside the Tactical Wallpaper Drawer**:
   - Tap any wallpaper card (`Cyber Neon HUD`, `Hex Titanium Camo`, `Kevlar Sovereign CBN`, or `OLED Tactical Stealth`).
   - The selected wallpaper will instantly apply to the live smartwatch dial backdrop with real-time blending and ambient glow.
3. **Wrist Lock Verification**:
   - The selected tactical wallpaper persists across wrist lock states, payment execution, and NQR code scanning.
